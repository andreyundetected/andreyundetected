## ⚡ About

At 16, I was the only person touching LLMs at the largest IT company in Montenegro - client requests involving AI just landed on my desk, because nobody else was looking closely at it yet. That habit stuck.

Since then: at a boutique AI studio, I built a **swarm of async agents** giving live, sourced meeting context to staff at the world's largest fintech-focused investment fund (used in 55%+ of their calls), a **hybrid vector+graph RAG engine** for the same fund's CRM that cut summarization from minutes to seconds, and a language-learning bot I grew from a solo prototype into **NeuroMentor** - a 7-person team and real investor negotiations.

I like problems where latency, correctness, and architecture all fight each other at once - and I like owning them end-to-end.
